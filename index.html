Around Anuradhapura

We're on the quiet side of Nuwara Wewa, off the tank road. Most of what you'll want is a short tuk-tuk ride, and the tank itself is at the end of the lane.

  • Sri Maha Bodhi10 min tuk-tuk · go early, before the crowds
  • Nuwara Wewa5 min walk · the tank, best at sunrise
  • Ruwanwelisaya12 min tuk-tuk · the white stupa, lit after dark
  • Mihintale25 min tuk-tuk · 1,840 steps, go at dawn
  • Isurumuniya15 min tuk-tuk · rock temple by the water

Getting here

Bandaranaike International Airport is about 180 km away, roughly 4 hours by road. There are also trains from Colombo Fort to Anuradhapura, and the station is 8 minutes from the house.

We don't arrange airport pickup, but a taxi or pre-booked driver can bring you straight here.
